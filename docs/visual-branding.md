# Visual branding

Solstice has its own application icon: a white wave on a black rounded
square. It is used for the program, the window and the taskbar. Windows may
continue to display an older cached icon after an update; signing out and in,
or rebuilding the icon cache, refreshes it.

The startup splash shows Solstice's own artwork: the Solstice logo over black
and white ink strokes, with the version and loading messages at the top right.

Solstice documents have their own icons: a white page with a folded corner
carrying the application icon, for `.kra`, and the same page labeled "KRZ"
for `.krz`. Explorer shows them only for files associated with Solstice;
starting Solstice from the extracted ZIP does not register the association.

Upstream project references, contributor credits, and third-party sponsor
attributions remain in place. Solstice is independently maintained and is
not affiliated with the Krita project.

Icons of other resource files (brush presets, bundles and others) are still
placeholders.
